Data source & methodology

Product: MODIS/Terra Land Surface Temperature & Emissivity (MOD11A2.061), variable LST_Day_1km; nominal spatial resolution 1 km; temporal composite 8 days. Annual temperature is the arithmetic mean of the available AppEEARS Statistics CSV records for each year. The Statistics CSV values are in Kelvin and are converted to °C by subtracting 273.15.

The dashboard combines the supplied metadata XML, Statistics CSV, QC-Day Statistics-QA CSV, and QC-Day lookup CSV datasets for the available study regions. The QC datasets support quality review and are not used to calculate the temperature trend.
